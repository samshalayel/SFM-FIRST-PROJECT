# Product Definition (PD) for Durban Beach Project

## Problem Statement
The Durban beach area currently faces challenges in managing visitor flow, safety, and facility maintenance effectively. There's a lack of real-time information for visitors and streamlined operations for staff.

## Proposed Solution
Develop a digital platform to enhance the beach experience for visitors and optimize management for authorities.

## Target Audience
- Visitors (tourists & locals)
- Beach Management Staff
- Lifeguards
- Local Businesses

## Key Features (Conceptual)
- Real-time crowd density & safety status
- Facility location & availability (showers, bins, etc.)
- Incident reporting for visitors
- Task management for maintenance & safety staff
- Weather & tide alerts

# Stage 0 (S0) Definition

## Core Actors
- Visitor
- Manager
- Lifeguard

## High-Level Modules
- Visitor App
- Management Dashboard
- Staff Mobile Interface

## Key Interactions
- Visitor checks information.
- Manager assigns maintenance task.
- Lifeguard updates status or reports incident.
- System sends emergency alerts.
